Global Recycle Standard

Recycled content, independently verified.

The Global Recycled Standard tracks and verifies the recycled content of a finished product, defining the requirements that make a recycled-content claim accurate.

The label carries strict guidelines and a certification process backed by independent certification of the input material, and it also addresses the social conditions of production.

Verified
Independent certification of inputs
Tracked
Chain of custody through the supply chain
Social
Labour and safety criteria included
Recycled Claim Standard

A claim you can follow back.

The Recycled Claim Standard is a voluntary international content claim standard that gives credibility to recycled material claims by tracking recycled input through the supply chain.

It applies chain of custody requirements, so the claim on the finished product can be traced back to the material it was made from.

Voluntary
International content claim standard
Input
Recycled material tracked from source
Custody
Chain of custody requirements applied

Need to know

Good to know.

What is the minimum order?

Most branded lines start at 25 units. Some stock items go lower and a few technical products start higher — the minimum is shown on every product page before you add it to a quote bag.

How fast can I get them?

Next working day on stock lines held in Bristol. Branded runs are 3–10 working days depending on print method. Tell us your date and we will confirm in writing before you commit.

Do I have to pay for a visual?

No. Send a logo in any format and you get a free artwork check plus a digital visual on the actual product within two working hours.

Where does the carbon figure come from?

Product weight and material from the manufacturer, print method from our own measured data, and transport from the actual route your order takes. Every line is broken out so you can check the arithmetic.

Can I see a sample first?

Yes. We send plain samples free on most lines, and branded pre-production samples on larger runs so you approve the real thing before the full run.
