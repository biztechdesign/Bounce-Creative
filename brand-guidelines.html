01 · LOGO GUIDELINES

The mark people remember.

The dot matrix is Bounce’s signature. Use the logo confidently, keep it intact, and always give it clear space.

Clear space

Keep at least the height of the dot grid clear on every side. Never crowd the wordmark.

Never redraw

Do not stretch, rotate, recolour, outline or rearrange the logo elements.

Two versions

Ink on light grounds. White on Ink or dark photography. Keep the red dots visible.

04 · IMAGERY

Useful, tactile, real.

Show products in the world they belong in: held, carried, used and chosen. Photography should feel warm, considered and tangible.

Show the use

Lead with the object in context. A product earns its place when people can imagine reaching for it.

Leave room for type

Choose calm areas, tonal depth and clean edges where copy needs to sit.

Keep it honest

Avoid glossy stock-photo perfection. Texture, hands and real materials make the brand credible.

07 · DO'S & DON'TS

Plain speaking, with a point of view.

Sound like a capable partner who knows the work. Be specific, warm and useful — never inflated.

Say this

“Send us your logo. We’ll come back with a print-accurate visual before you commit.”

Not this

“Unlock next-level brand experiences through our revolutionary merchandise ecosystem.”

01A · LOGO BACKGROUNDS

Choose the right ground.

Use the ink mark on white and quiet light surfaces. Use the white mark on Ink, dark photography and high-contrast brand surfaces.

Light surface

Ink logo with full-colour red dot matrix. Best for headers, documents and presentations.

Dark surface

White logo with red dot matrix. Best for the footer, announcement areas and dark campaign panels.

Photography

Place the logo over a calm, uncluttered area. Add a scrim when legibility is not guaranteed.

01B · LOGO MISUSE

Keep the mark intact.

Never stretch, rotate, recolour, outline, shadow, separate or rearrange the dot matrix and wordmark.

Do not distort

Never stretch, compress, skew or tilt the logo in any direction.

Do not decorate

No drop shadows, glows, bevels, outlines or unapproved colour changes.

Do not clash

Never place the logo on a busy, low-contrast or Bounce Red background.

01C · FAVICON & APP ICON

The dots, compacted.

Use the dot matrix symbol alone where the full wordmark would become illegible: browser tabs, app icons, social avatars and small product labels.

16 × 16

Smallest browser favicon. Use the dot matrix mark only.

32–48px

Standard browser tab, shortcut and desktop contexts.

180–512px

Apple Touch, app store and device home-screen exports. Supply SVG and PNG.

05 · ICONOGRAPHY

Light, linear signals.

Use the Solar Linear icon family already present in the homepage. Icons clarify meaning; they never replace a label.

Grid & stroke

24 × 24 grid, 1.5px native stroke, round caps and joins. Use 16, 20, 24, 32 or 48px sizes.

Colour rules

Ink by default, Bounce Red for action, Eco Green for leaf/sustainability meaning, white on dark surfaces.

Never lose meaning

Do not scale below 14px, mix icon families, or communicate status by colour alone.

09 · PRACTICAL RULES

Make the system hold.

A short checklist for every designer, developer and partner working with Bounce.

Do

Use approved logo files. Maintain clear space. Keep text at AA contrast. Follow the type scale. Use visible focus rings. Keep tap targets touch-friendly. Use semantic colour with a text label.

Don't

Don't freestyle font sizes, introduce extra accent colours, use all-caps for paragraphs, remove focus rings, use placeholder text as a label, or put two competing primary CTAs side by side.

10 · IMPLEMENTATION SPEC

The details that keep it consistent.

Use this as the handoff sheet for digital products, campaign pages, documents and supplier artwork.

Assets & placement

SVG for digital, PNG for web/docs, PDF for print and EPS for suppliers. Place the full logo top-left in headers, centred on covers, or at the bottom of email signatures.

Buttons & links

Primary, outline, ghost and text-link variants. Use 10px radius, 44px minimum touch target, 15px semibold label, 8px icon gap, and visible red focus ring. States: default, hover, active, disabled and focus.

Surfaces & motion

White and Band for reading surfaces; Ink for emphasis. Use restrained transitions around .18s–.3s ease and disable non-essential motion for reduced-motion users.

Gradients

Photography and flat colour do the expressive work. If a gradient is needed, use Ink → Bounce Red for a dark CTA surface; never add unapproved decorative gradients.

Responsive rules

Desktop content max-width 1440px with generous section rhythm. At 900px, collapse the rail into a horizontal scrollable index. At 560px, use one-column cards and 44px minimum controls.

Application surfaces

Shop: product-led and scannable. Corporate gifting: editorial and consultative. Sustainability: evidence-led, with measured CO₂e and certification clearly labelled.
