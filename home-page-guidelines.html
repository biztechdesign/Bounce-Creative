Foundation

Two colours carry the identity.

Ink and Bounce Red do the work. Everything else is ground — quiet, warm neutrals that exist so the two brand colours land. Green is reserved: it marks eco, and nothing else.

The one rule that is easy to get wrong. Bounce Red at #E50E46 reaches only 4.26:1 on the Band ground — below the 4.5:1 minimum for text. Use #E50E46 for fills, icons and rules, and #C40A39 whenever red carries words. The darker tint clears the threshold on both grounds (6.09:1 on Paper, 5.53:1 on Band) and is close enough that the two read as the same brand red side by side.

11.5px is the floor. WCAG sets no minimum font size, so this is a house rule rather than a conformance one. Nothing on the page goes below it, and anything under 18.66px bold or 24px regular must still clear 4.5:1 contrast — which is most of the page.

Components

Clear actions, quiet surfaces.

Only two things on this page are fully round: buttons and chips. Everything else uses one of three radii. There are no drop shadows and no cards — separation comes from hairlines and space.

Non-negotiable

Accessibility is part of the brand.

These are not suggestions layered on afterwards — the page was audited to WCAG 2.2 AA and these rules are what keep it there. Breaking one is a brand defect, not just a technical one.

Red on text#C40A39, never #E50E46 — the brand red fails on Band
Contrast floor4.5:1 for text under 18.66px bold / 24px regular; 3:1 above
Text on photographyA scrim strong enough at the text’s own position — measure, do not assume
FocusAlways visible. Never outline:none without a replacement
Sticky headerscroll-padding-top must match its height, or focus hides beneath it
Auto-motionAnything moving past 5s needs a pause control on the page
Targets24×24px, or 24px of clear space around smaller ones
HeadingsOne h1, no skipped levels — h2 never jumps to h4
LandmarksAll content inside header, main, footer, nav or aside
ReflowNo horizontal scroll at 320px — test every new section
In practice

Getting it right.

Do

  • Let space separate things. Hairlines and generous padding, not boxes.
  • Break headings deliberately. Two or three short lines beat one long one.
  • Keep red rare. One red element per view is usually enough.
  • Photograph the product in use. Real items, real light, room for type.
  • Alternate grounds. Paper, then Band, so long pages stay navigable.
  • State the number. “2,100 products”, “in 2 hours” — specifics carry the brand.

Don't

  • No drop shadows. The page has four, all inset rings. Keep it that way.
  • No third typeface, and no third brand colour.
  • Never set #E50E46 as text on the Band ground.
  • No green outside eco. It is a signal, not a palette colour.
  • No two Band sections in a row. The rhythm stops working.
  • No type below 11.5px, and nothing important in uppercase micro-labels.
